Patched for archival, not the original deploy:
  - BrowserRouter -> MemoryRouter, so the archive cannot write to the parent's
    URL or hijack the visitor's Back button from inside the iframe.
  - index.html and blog.html dropped from the build; both are
    <meta http-equiv="refresh"> shells pointing at /react.html, which Vite never
    rewrites. react.html is the real entry and was renamed to index.html.
  - sw.js and manifest.json deleted, and their <link> tags stripped.
  - AnimatePresence mode="wait" -> sync, and MotionSection's whileInView ->
    animate. Both gated whether content mounted or became visible on an
    animation completing (also in ServicesPage, AboutPage, UpdatesPage and
    HomePage). In the frame that left Projects and About rendering
    nothing at all: the nav updated, the page stayed blank. Appearance is
    unchanged; only the trigger is.
