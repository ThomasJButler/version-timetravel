Patched for archival, not the original deploy, following the same rules as v5:
  - BrowserRouter -> MemoryRouter, so the archive cannot write to the parent's
    URL or hijack the visitor's Back button from inside the iframe.
  - Client build only. The full build also runs an SSR pass and a prerender
    step, which produce per-route static HTML that is redundant inside a frame
    driven by MemoryRouter.
  - sw.js and manifest.json deleted, and the manifest link stripped. They came
    from the app's own public/ and precache root-absolute paths on the shared
    thomasjbutler.github.io origin.
  - blog.html and services.html dropped. Both are meta-refresh redirect shells
    pointing at root-absolute paths, which resolve to the wrong origin from
    inside the archive. Their in-app routes still work under MemoryRouter.

Kept deliberately: the og-*.png social images and src/content/blog/*.md, as in
v5. Neither is ever rendered, both are part of the real build, and the rule here
is to patch only what actively breaks the archive.
