Patched for archival, not the original deploy:
  - BrowserRouter -> MemoryRouter, so the archive cannot write to the parent's
    URL or hijack the visitor's Back button from inside the iframe.
  - blog.html dropped from the build. Unlike v3.5 and v4, index.html here is the
    real entry and carries no meta-refresh shell, so nothing had to be renamed.
  - Client build only. The full build also runs an SSR pass and a prerender
    step, which produce per-route static HTML that is redundant inside a frame
    driven by MemoryRouter.
  - sw.js and manifest.json deleted, and the manifest link stripped. They came
    from the app's own public/ and precache root-absolute paths on the shared
    thomasjbutler.github.io origin.

Kept deliberately: the og-*.png social images, roughly 1.3MB. They are never
rendered, but they are part of the real build and harmless, and the rule here is
to patch only what actively breaks the archive.
