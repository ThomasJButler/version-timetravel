Patched for archival, not the original deploy:
  - BrowserRouter -> MemoryRouter, so the archive cannot write to the parent's
    URL or hijack the visitor's Back button from inside the iframe.
  - index.html and blog.html dropped from the build. Both are
    <meta http-equiv="refresh"> shells pointing at /react.html, which Vite never
    rewrites, so leaving them in would redirect the frame to the live site.
    react.html is the real entry and was renamed to index.html.
  - @vitejs/plugin-legacy removed; it roughly doubles the output for no
    archival value.
  - sw.js and manifest.json deleted, and their <link> tags stripped. They came
    from the app's own public/ and precache root-absolute paths on the shared
    thomasjbutler.github.io origin.
